Overview

A more organized way to handle reports.

Reports can be difficult to track when they are spread across messages. CASE FINDER is built to give each submission a clear reference and a consistent review process.

01 / INTAKE

Report submission

Collect the details of a concern through the bot’s reporting flow, rather than relying on scattered moderator messages.

02 / REVIEW

Moderator review

Give authorized moderators a structured record to review, update, and manage as they assess a report.

03 / FOLLOW-UP

Case references

Use a case reference to make follow-up and status checks easier to identify and discuss privately.

The process

From submission to decision.

A straightforward workflow helps moderators handle each report consistently and keep a record of the steps taken.

STEP 01

Submit a report

A community member provides the information moderators need to understand the concern.

STEP 02

Review the details

Authorized moderators examine the submission and decide what, if anything, needs to happen next.

STEP 03

Record the outcome

Case notes and status changes help keep the review process clear for the people authorized to see it.

Moderation standards

A report is a starting point, not a verdict.

Every submission should be reviewed on its own information. CASE FINDER is intended to support responsible moderation, not public accusations or automatic judgments.

— Keep case information within the appropriate moderation team.
— Review the available information before making a decision.
— Do not publish private details or identify reporters publicly.
— Record moderation actions clearly and consistently.
