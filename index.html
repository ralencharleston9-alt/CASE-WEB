How it works

A straightforward process.

CASE FINDER uses a command-based interaction to provide reporting information.

STEP 01

Start with !report

Enter the reporting command in a server where CASE FINDER is available.

STEP 02

Receive instructions

CASE FINDER sends the reporting instructions directly to the person initiating the process.

STEP 03

Case information

CASE FINDER sends a direct message containing case information to the Discord account associated with the entered user ID.

Privacy Policy

Information about CASE FINDER's current reporting interactions.

1. Introduction

CASE FINDER is a Discord bot designed to provide reporting instructions and case-related information. This policy explains its currently described reporting interactions.

2. Information involved

The reporting process involves Discord user IDs entered by users and the Discord accounts involved in the interaction. Discord may independently process information under its own privacy policy.

3. How the reporting process works

  • A user starts the process using !report.
  • The reporting instructions are sent to the reporter.
  • Case information is sent by direct message to the account associated with the entered user ID.

4. Information storage

This policy does not claim that CASE FINDER maintains a persistent case database or permanent report history. Information storage and retention should be reviewed against the bot's actual implementation.

5. Third-party services

CASE FINDER operates through Discord. Its interactions are subject to Discord's platform terms and privacy practices.

6. Contact

For questions about CASE FINDER, contact the community administrator responsible for the server where the bot is being used.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when CASE FINDER's features or data-handling practices change.
